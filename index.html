Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ATS CV Maker.

What is an ATS CV?

An ATS (Applicant Tracking System) CV uses clean formatting, standard section headers, and parseable typography so automated recruitment software can read your qualifications without errors.

Are the PDF Tools included in the app?

Yes! You can merge supporting certificates or ID documents with your CV, compress PDF files, or extract specific pages directly within ATS CV Maker.

Is my personal data stored securely?

100%! All your CV profiles and details are stored locally on your device with hardware encryption. We do not upload your personal resume details to external databases.
